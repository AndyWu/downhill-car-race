# CRITIQUE REPORT — ROUND 4
**Benchmark Comparison:** The Last of Us Series (Visual Weathering, PBR Mud/Water, Tactile Physics)
**Overall Build Score:** 13 / 20  (Round 3: 12 / 20 — +1: networking, AI ordering, controls and chase cam measurably fixed; held back by a regressed berm, unchanged start-zone geometry, an inconsistent lens-mud effect and leftover phantom water drops)

## 1. Scene & Environment (2-Mile Country Dirt Road) [ Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The berm regressed under sustained steering. Steered drift runs now go **15.92 m** (s=600, steer +0.35) and **19.58 m** (s=2200, steer +0.5) beyond the road edge, against R3's 3.16/3.23 m. Only s=1500 stays contained (2.38 m). The no-input case improved: the 30 s no-input run peaks at 1.37 m beyond the edge, and in the 90 s ai=5 race the no-input player was off-road 18 s (R3: 85 s). But it then sat *on the road* (lateral 0–3.4) from t=50 to t=90 at 0.1–1.3 m/s, 42 slow-seconds, s=572→602. It doesn't "keep rolling"; it parks on a flat or uphill section with no gradient to coast down. `mud-splatter-rendered-2.jpg` and `hazard-mud-after.jpg` show the truck climbing the right-hand bank with the horizon rolled about 15°.
- **Failure 2:** The far-LOD change hasn't moved the start-zone budget. `renderer.info` at q=default, ai=5: **s=0: 221 calls / 360,191 tris** (R3: 223 / 366,871). s=600: 103 / 179,814; s=1500: 91 / 136,888 (R3 168,732, −19 %); s=3150: 74 / 152,628 (R3 189,500, −19 %). Switching AI trucks to far-LOD beyond 33 m only pays off mid-course. The start line, where all six cars and the bales, bunting and crowd sit within 33 m, is unchanged, and the R3 routing target (≤ 120 calls / 200k) was not met.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/scene/ground.js`, make berm containment velocity-aware. Above 6 m/s lateral speed, increase the inner-face slope, and add a lateral restoring acceleration of 2 m/s² × (|lateral| − hw) in `surfaceAt`'s friction output, capped at hw + 4. Add a `sanity.mjs` case asserting max excursion < 5 m for steer ±0.5 at 10 m/s. For the stall on flat sections, audit `track.js` grade at s≈570–600 and enforce a minimum −1.5 % downhill grade along the whole centreline. In `src/scene/scenery.js`, instance the start-zone hay bales, crowd and bunting (one `InstancedMesh` each) and cut bale radial segments to 12.

## 2. Network, Offline PWA & QR-WebRTC Star Topology [Opus 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Under loss, the client player falls far behind. In the real-game lossy run (`tests/net-race.mjs` "lossy link (15 % loss, 20–80 ms jitter on 'fast')"), after 11 s the host car was at s=138.6 and the *client's* car only at **28.3 m** (host simulation) / 28.6 m (client). The same test without loss had the client at 52.7 m. The host jitter buffer reports "lost 33, strokes recovered 5": paddle strokes are impulses, and losing one costs the player permanently. The client-predicted pop is also visible: p95 **19.34 cm per frame**, max 21.07 cm (loopback R3 reported 3.62 cm max). Upload is fixed: real-game client tx is **6,100 B in 10.01 s = 609 B/s** (R3: 7.5 KB/s), host 2,546 B/s per client at 14.2 B/car.
- **Failure 2:** Offline is now proven on the real build. `net-offline.mjs` on the real dist reported 17 precache entries / 1,145 KiB, the manifest inlined into `dist/sw.js`, offline-ready fired only after `statechange → activated`, 17/17 cached, and offline reload plus deep link both boot with 0 failed requests. Loopback 5 %/15 % loss passes (202/179 snapshots, interp delay 126/133 ms). The remaining gap is the unit of loss above: inputs are sent at 30 Hz with 2-frame redundancy, so a lost *pair* of packets (about 2 % at 15 % loss, i.e. once a second) drops a paddle stroke entirely.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/net/sync.js`, send paddle strokes as a *cumulative* counter (`uint8 strokeSeqL/R` + last impulse) inside every input packet, so the host replays any missed strokes from the counter delta. That makes stroke loss impossible while the link is up. Extend `tests/net-race.mjs` (Network-owned) to assert client s within 10 % of a no-loss baseline under 15 % loss. Clamp `predictor` correction to ≤ 5 cm per frame with exponential smoothing (τ = 120 ms).

## 3. Core Gameplay, Controls & Flag Physics [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The paddle advantage is lopsided. With the R3 regression fixed, paddling is now deterministic and never goes backwards: four repeated 10 s paddle runs from v=2 gave Δs = 105.7 / 111.5 / 111.5 / 111.5 m with min Δs 0 (R3: −64.7 m). But paddling now covers **3.6×** the distance of coasting (111.5 m vs 30.7 m, 15.58 vs 3.93 m/s). In the in-game race, a no-input player still led every AI at t=20–40 (s=219/368/515 vs best AI 203/315/443) purely on gravity, then stalled. Either the downhill feels like a motor or the flats feel like a wall; neither reads as a heavy wooden cart.
- **Failure 2:** The headlight tap is fixed: on→off→on over three taps (`[true,false,true]`, logs `held 4425 allowed 600` → `->true`, `held 2445 allowed 4641` → `->false`), and the controls-phase tap also toggled (`headlightAfterTap:true`). The touch handbrake is now brutal: holding the zone took 11.29 → **0.02 m/s in 1.5 s (≈7.5 m/s²)**, while the debug handbrake=1 path measures 3.07 m/s². Two different decel figures for the same lever mean the touch path stacks something extra, probably the touch drag mapping to >1 or the reverse gear engaging.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/core/physics.js`, scale gravity-along-slope by a rolling-resistance term `rollingResist·m·g·(1 + 0.02·v)` so that free coasting peaks at about 10 m/s on the steepest grade. Reduce `paddleEfficiency` by 25 % so paddling roughly doubles coasting rather than nearly quadrupling it. In `src/core/input.js`, clamp `touchHB` to [0, 1] after the drag mapping and ignore gear-zone pointers that started in the handbrake zone. Add to `tests/core-r4.mjs` an assertion that touch and debug handbrake decel agree within 15 %.

## 4. Audio & Diegetic Cockpit UI [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The results-card projection contradicts the race. At the player's finish (1:37.35) the card lists "Ace ~158 s, Maeve ~197 s, Buster ~200 s, Barnaby ~213 s, **Clementine ~352 s**". But at t=90 in the same race Clementine was at s=885, *ahead* of Barnaby at 842, and running 11.7 m/s. The ~352 s projection (139 s slower than Barnaby) is wrong by more than 2 minutes, which suggests it extrapolates from a momentary speed dip. The AI order on the card is otherwise correct now (R3 had Ace 3rd of the AIs).
- **Failure 2:** The glyph idle-flash still fires *inside* the race: opacity 0.32 at t=0, 0 at 5 and 10 s, **0.26 at 15 s and 20 s**, then 0 through 60 s. That is within the "pre-first-stroke, < 20 s" rule, but a coasting player sees the arcs reappear over the road 15 s into the run. Audio sand/ambient was not re-measured this round (`tests/audio-metrics.mjs` was not part of the surviving batch), so R3's sand-brighter-than-gravel finding stands unverified as fixed.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ui/index.js`, compute the "still rolling" projection as `raceTime + (TRACK_LENGTH − s) / max(4, avgSpeedLast20s)`, using a 20 s moving average from the vehicle's s history, not instantaneous speed. Suppress `idleFlash` entirely once `raceTime > 8 s`. In `src/audio/bank.js`, re-run the sand low-pass (target centroid 1.2–1.8 kHz) and record the result in `tests/audio-metrics.mjs` output with an assertion.

After driving through mud, the paddle bases pick up a darker gritty band (`mud-splatter-rendered.jpg`, `hazard-mud-after.jpg`), but the lever knobs and wheel rim stay clean. There is no wet sheen on the coat, so it reads as dust, not mud.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/vehicles/models_a.js`/`models_b.js`, remove the duplicate rear-plate decal mesh on trucks 5 and 6 (or give the second one `polygonOffset` and the same text). Source both flag and plate text from `spec.raceNumber`. In `src/vehicles/kit.js`, make `mudCoat` also drive roughness down to 0.35 for the first 4 s after contact (wet) before drying to 0.95, and apply it to the knob and wheel-rim materials.

## 6. Graphics, Lighting & PBR Shaders [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Lens mud swings between none and huge blobs. Driving through mud with renders every 1/30 s (`mud-splatter-rendered.jpg`, 90 frames) leaves **no visible lens mud at all**. The `scene=mud` run advanced in 0.25 s steps (`hazard-mud-after.jpg`) shows about 10 large translucent brown teardrops, one reaching y≈230 (upper third), each with an identical white specular dot. The spawn rate depends on how often `render()` sees the wet latch, not on the mud contact itself. Phantom water drops on dry road persist: `cockpit-midtrack-speed.jpg` (teleport to s=1500, dry dirt) shows 4–5 clear droplets on the wheel hub and lower cockpit.
- **Failure 2:** Gravel and chase cam are fixed and the tiers improved. Gravel now reads as loose stones scattered over dirt (`hazard-gravel.jpg`), not R3's cobble paving. The chase cam follows: `chase-ai-race.jpg` frames the green "13" truck about 8 m ahead, where R3 left the camera at the start. FPS in SwiftShader: q-low **2.0**, chase **2.0** (R3: 1.8 / 1.0). q-medium and q-default were not re-measured in the surviving runs. The start-line 360k triangles (§1) remain the dominant cost.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/graphics/postfx.js`, move mud-drop spawning from `render()` into a per-physics-step accumulator (`spawnBudget += dt · rate(speed)` while `surface==='mud'`). Flush it at the next render so the spawn count is frame-rate independent. Cap drop radius at 0.035 and randomise the specular position and size per drop. Clear water drops on `teleport()` and require `surface in {'water','rutpool','puddle'}` for ≥ 2 physics steps before spawning any.

## 7. The 5 Tiered AI Agents [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Tiers 3 and 4 still swap. The in-game 90 s ai=5 race finished `Ace 1045 > Buster 944 > Maeve 909 > Clementine 885 > Barnaby 842`, with Buster (tier 3) 35 m ahead of Maeve (tier 4). `tests/ai-game.mjs` reports the same: `Ace 1040 > Buster 940 > Maeve 904 > Clementine 880 > Barnaby 838`. `ai-sim 3` passed all assertions, with averages Ace 260.0 < Maeve 295.9 < Buster 308.0 < Clementine 318.3 < Barnaby 342.5, but seed 3 has Buster at 303.7 s beating Maeve at 305.3 s, and its s@90 shows Maeve *last* (835). With signature trucks Maeve now drives the slower `rust-bucket`, which erases her skill edge.
- **Failure 2:** Ace walks away and the pack doesn't race. There were only **2 AI lead changes** in 90 s (Barnaby → Buster → Ace by t≈60), and then Ace led by 101 m at t=90 at 13.6 m/s while the rest sat at 10.9–12.1 m/s. Stalls are almost gone (slow-seconds: Barnaby 4, Maeve 2, the others 0; off-road ≤ 2 s each), a clear gain over R3. But a 101 m gap after 90 s means the second half of a 4–5 min race has no visible contest.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ai/profiles.js`, set Maeve `speed.cap` +1.5 and `paddle.strength` ×1.08 to offset `rust-bucket`'s `dragCoef`, and gate the assertion in `tests/ai-game.mjs` on Maeve ahead of Buster at t=90. In `src/ai/driver.js`, have the existing soft rubber band also cap the leader: when more than 60 m ahead, multiply the leader's paddle cadence by 0.85 (`draft.leaderEase`). Target ≥ 4 lead changes in 90 s in `ai-game.mjs`.
