## 1. Scene & Environment (2-Mile Country Dirt Road) [ Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Water hazards don't read as water. `hazard-puddle.jpg` shows a flat pale-blue decal with no reflection of the trees or poles beside it, no wet darkening of the dirt around it, and no meniscus. `hazard-stream.jpg` is a thin blue strip level with the road: no cut banks, no ford stones, no culvert or plank bridge. Nothing tells the player that water crosses the road here. TLOU puddles reflect their surroundings and soak into the ground around them; these look like stickers.
- **Failure 2:** The world has no boundary and no story. In `ai-race-60s.jpg` and `controls-tilt.jpg` the player (no input) rolled 40.6 m off the centreline (`aiRace` t=50 lat=40.6) into open field and forest, with nothing stopping it. `?scene=barn` (`barn-cockpit.jpg`, `barn-chase.jpg`) frames only a mailbox. The only barn seen is a distant red box (`hazard-puddle.jpg`, top right). Foliage is low-poly cards and trees repeat (identical canopies in `chase-ai-race.jpg`). There is no weathering story: no ruts filled with water, no fallen fence sections, no tyre-worn verges.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/scene/farms.js`, place each farm's barn so that it sits 25–40 m from the road on the outside of a bend (set `farm.s` to the bend apex), and make `sceneShortcutS('barn')` in main use `farm.s - 60` with the barn in the view frustum. In `src/scene/hazards.js`, give `stream` a 0.4 m-deep cut with sloped banks in `terrain.js` heightAt, plus a plank bridge or ford-stone mesh. Add a continuous verge berm/ditch (`ground.js`, +0.5 m lip at |lateral| > width/2 + 1.5) and fence posts with gaps in `scenery.js`, so that leaving the road means grinding into a ditch, not driving freely across a meadow.

## 2. Network, Offline PWA & QR-WebRTC Star Topology [Opus 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The service worker was still `"activating"` (not `activated`) when the test checked it. That was after `navigator.serviceWorker.ready`, which normally resolves only once a worker is activated, so the result is unexpected and needs explaining. The precache `sbd-precache-c2ebf5564288` held only 16 entries, and there was no runtime cache. Offline reload and offline `?autostart=1` both booted (`offline-boot.jpg`), so it works, but only because install fetched everything first. There is no guard against a half-activated worker, and the page never waits for `activated` before telling the user "ready to play offline".
- **Failure 2:** Loopback and two-page races pass (offer and answer are 104 chars each, built from 78 B binary; 180/180 inputs and snapshots; RTT 0.70 ms; net-race host and client agree within ~1 m). However, the host snapshot is 202 B × 60 Hz = 12.1 KB/s per client, and for 5 clients that is about 61 KB/s, unordered with `maxRetransmits 0`. The test only proves this on localhost. Separately, the lobby screens (`lobby-host.jpg`, `lobby-join.jpg`) show steps 1–4 already struck through before the user has done anything, and they show a red "camera could not start (Requested device not found)" error on the *host* screen, which only needs to display a QR at that point.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/net/sync.js`, quantise snapshots: positions as int16 relative to the track `s`, quaternions as smallest-three 10-bit, speed as uint8. Send at 30 Hz with delta flags. Target ≤ 64 B per car, under 25 KB/s for 6 cars. In `src/net/lobby.js` / `lobbyView.js`, defer `getUserMedia` on the host until the "scan answer" step and tick checklist steps only on user confirmation. In `src/net/pwa.js`, expose `offlineReady` only after `registration.active.state === 'activated'` and the precache count equals the number of files in the manifest.

## 3. Core Gameplay, Controls & Flag Physics [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The handbrake has no tactile weight. With the handbrake zone held at full travel (lever rotX 0.70) for 1.5 s, speed went from 7.13 to 7.05 m/s, a loss of 0.05 m/s². A wooden friction block on a downhill should scrub roughly 2–4 m/s² and judder. A tap in the headlight zone (x=1000, y=850) failed to toggle the light in 2 of 2 runs (`headlightAfterTap:false`). The 350 ms tap window measured with `performance.now()` is fragile when frames are slow. The gear drag did work (`reverse:true`).
- **Failure 2:** The tilt mapping is correct once calibrated (±28.6° → steer ±0.61 → wheel rotZ ∓1.27). But before `calibrate()`, an upright landscape pose with `screen.orientation.angle = 0` reads raw −90° and gives a full −1 steer lock (`tilt.upright`), and the car drove straight into the field (`controls-tilt.jpg`). Swipe paddling works but is weak. In 4 s real time, one swipe pair gave a +4.07 m/s gain against +2.91 m/s from gravity alone. Meanwhile the no-input player car reached s=580 at 60 s, ahead of every AI. Gravity dominates and paddling barely matters.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/core/physics.js` `step()`, model the handbrake as a Coulomb drag of `brakeForce * handbrake * 0.6 * g * mass` opposing velocity on the rear contacts, plus a 12 Hz ±3 % normal-force judder. In `src/core/input.js`, auto-`calibrate()` on the first 10 motion samples at race start. Treat a pointer as a tap based on `moved < 24 && (e.timeStamp - p.t0) < 450`, using event timestamps rather than `performance.now()`. Scale `emitStroke` strength by `1 + 0.5*(1 - speed/12)` so that paddling clearly beats coasting at low speed.

## 4. Audio & Diegetic Cockpit UI [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The "zero HUD, glyphs fade after 3 s" contract is broken. Large white swipe arcs, the handbrake glyph, the bulb and the F/R glyphs are fully opaque in `cockpit-midtrack-speed.jpg`, `hazard-mud.jpg`, `golden.jpg`, `ai-race-60s.jpg` (60 s simulated) and `chase-ai-race.jpg`. They cover about 15 % of the frame over the road at all times.
- **Failure 2:** There is no diegetic race information. The finish (`finish.jpg`: finished, place 1, 65.5 s, mode 'finish') shows an ordinary stretch of road with no finish arch, no flagman and no crowd, and nothing on the truck such as a chalk board or tally. The same "7" flag is mounted dead-centre in the sightline on every truck (every cockpit shot) and blows out to white at night (`night.jpg`). Audio is all oscillator and noise synthesis (`src/audio/index.js`: createOscillator/sawtooth creak). It has no recorded gravel or wood samples, so it cannot carry the TLOU-style tactile foley.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ui/index.js`, drive glyph opacity with a timer that starts at the race GO, not at the last touch (or that is reset only by touches in that zone), and fade it to 0 over 3 s. In `src/core/flag.js`, use the truck's slot number and colour from the spec, and move `flagMount` 0.35 m to the side (handled in vehicles). Clamp the flag material's `emissive` to 0 and its tone to below 0.8 albedo. In `src/audio/index.js`, add 3–4 short CC0 one-shot samples (gravel crunch, plank thump, mud squelch), pitched and granulated by speed and surface.

## 5. The 10 Homemade Soap Box Trucks [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The trucks can't be told apart in play. In `trucks-contact-sheet.jpg` (10 chase shots, `truck-0..9-chase.jpg`), each truck is a roughly 30-px blob 25 m ahead of the camera, so all ten silhouettes look identical. Only the garage shows any detail (`garage.jpg`, Barrel Bomber), and the near-identical stat bars there (5 of 6 at about 55 %) hide the differences between trucks.
- **Failure 2:** The cockpit parts are untextured primitives. The paddles are flat yellow, blue or pink boxes with a brown stripe (every cockpit shot). The lever knobs are red octagons, and the steering wheel is a smooth torus with grey box spokes (`cockpit-day-start.jpg`). None of them shows grain, chipped paint, rust, tape or hand wear, against a benchmark where every surface is weathered. The night shot (`night.jpg`) shows the rusted-metal texture on truck 2 smeared and stretched at the nose.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/vehicles/parts.js`, build the paddles from `materials.woodWeathered`, with bevelled `ExtrudeGeometry`, a gaffer-tape band (`rope`/cloth material) and per-truck `paint` colour run through a wear mask (edge-AO multiplied by noise). Replace the wheel spokes with tapered cylinders and wrap the rim in tape. Fix the UVs on the barrel and nose meshes in `models_a.js`/`models_b.js` using box-projected `worldUV`. Widen the stat spread in `TRUCKS` so each truck has at least one stat ≥ 80 % and one ≤ 30 %.

## 6. Graphics, Lighting & PBR Shaders [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Performance in SwiftShader is extremely heavy: 0.6 FPS for the chase cam with 6 cars and 0.4 FPS even at `q=low` (`cockpit-lowq.jpg`), at 1366×1024. SwiftShader is CPU-rendered, but `q=low` being *slower* than the chase view shows that the low tier doesn't cut the expensive passes (postfx splatter, bloom, planar reflection, shadows). Low quality also renders gravel as flat white noise that reads as salt or snow (`cockpit-lowq.jpg`, `hazard-gravel.jpg`).
- **Failure 2:** The mud is not PBR mud. `hazard-mud.jpg` is darker speckled dirt with a few specular dots. It has no wet sheen that changes with roughness, no ruts holding standing water, no displacement and no tyre-track deformation. After driving through it (`hazard-mud-after.jpg`) the truck carries no mud at all. The lens splatter exists (`postfx.js addSplatter`), but `main.js` only arms it inside `render()` when `ps.surface==='mud'` on that exact frame, so with `advance()` or any dropped frame it never fires. The dusk sky shows full star fields in a pink sky (`cockpit-dusk-headlight.jpg`). At night the bloom blows the flag out to white (`night.jpg`).
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/graphics/materials.js`, give `M.mud` a wetness uniform. Lerp roughness from 0.9 to 0.25 and darken albedo by 0.55 in the rut mask, and use a parallax-occlusion pass (or vertex displacement on the mud patch geometry) of 4–6 cm. Add a `mudCoat` uniform to the truck materials (a world-space noise mask that rises from the wheel arches), increased by the physics state. In `main.js`, latch `wetHit = true` inside `fixedUpdate` and consume it in `render()`. In `src/graphics/postfx.js`/`renderer.js`, make `q=low` disable bloom, reflection and splatter refraction, and use 0.7× the render scale. In `sky.js`, fade the star alpha to 0 at the dusk preset.

## 7. The 5 Tiered AI Agents [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** An AI gets stuck in the in-game race (ai=5, 60 s simulated). Buster (tier 3) is at s=136→144→153→159→166 from 20 to 60 s, at 0.1–1.4 m/s, so effectively stopped for 40 s. Maeve has dropped to 1.1 m/s at s=524 by 60 s. The coasting player with *zero input* leads at s=580 even while running 40 m off-road. The headless sim doesn't show these stalls, so it disagrees with the game.
- **Failure 2:** The tiers are not monotonic. In `ai-sim.mjs 2 --real`, Buster (tier 3, 289.7 s) beats Maeve (tier 4, 293.3 s) and has the most hazard hits of all five (19.5, including 11.5 water). Barnaby's 472 s is 1.75× Ace's 269.7 s, so he will never be in the same race on screen.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ai/driver.js`, add stuck recovery. If `speed < 1.5` for 2 s at `s > 20`, set reverse for 0.8 s with the opposite steer, then resume, and log it. Make the in-game test identical to the sim by running `createAIDriver` through `tests/ai-sim.mjs --real` with the full `createVehicleBody` and the start ramp. In `src/ai/profiles.js`, give Maeve `speed.cap` 19 and `hazardWeight.water` 14 vs Buster's 8, and rubber-band Barnaby to within 1.3× of the leader's time.
