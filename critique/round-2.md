In `src/scene/ground.js`, raise the berm to +0.6 m with a 35° inner slope at |lateral| > hw + 1.2, and add a lateral restoring force to `surfaceAt` (`grass.drag` ×3 beyond hw + 4). Limit bunting and crowd (`scenery.js`) to s < 120 and s > 3050.

## 2. Network, Offline PWA & QR-WebRTC Star Topology [Opus 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The bandwidth target is met only on paper. `net-loopback` reports v2 snapshots at 30 Hz, 158 B for 6 cars (≈26 B per car, not the claimed ~22), 4.65 KiB/s per client and 23.3 KiB/s for 5 clients. That is under the 25 KiB/s gate by just 1.7 KiB/s, and it was measured on localhost with 0 % loss. Nothing tests jitter or loss, and with `maxRetransmits 0` that is exactly what an iPad hotspot will produce. Client prediction is asserted (net-race: host 130.3 m vs client-own 130.5 m, PASS), but only for a straight 10 s run with no packet loss.
- **Failure 2:** The service worker still reports `activating` right after `serviceWorker.ready` and reaches `activated` 3 s later (`swState:"activating", swAfter3s:"activated"`, cache `sbd-precache-68851d033f35`, 17 entries). Offline boot and autostart pass (`offline-boot.jpg`). The lobby is fixed: `lobby-host.jpg` shows no pre-ticked steps (R1 had four struck through), and loopback confirms `getUserMedia calls: 0` until "Scan reply" is tapped. No test asserts that the UI's offline-ready mark waits for `activated`.
- **Concrete Fix:** Add a loss/jitter stage to `tests/net-loopback.mjs`: drop 10 % and delay 0–80 ms in a shim around `fastChannel.send`. Assert the interpolator error is under 0.5 m and that client prediction reconciles within 200 ms. In `src/net/sync.js`, send keyframes every 15th snapshot and pack each car as `s:uint16(0.05 m) + lateral:int8 + yaw:int8 + speed:uint8 + flags:uint8` (6 B instead of 26). In `src/net/pwa.js`, resolve `offlineReady` from the worker's `statechange` event to `activated`, and add a Playwright assertion for it.

## 3. Core Gameplay, Controls & Flag Physics [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The headlight tap zone still doesn't respond. A synthetic touch tap at the zone centre (861, 680 → nx 0.73, ny 0.83) left `headlightAfterTap:false` in 3 of 3 runs across R1 and R2, while a gear drag in the adjacent zone toggled reverse correctly (`reverseAfterGearDragDown:true`). The handbrake is fixed: debug handbrake=1 from 11.92 m/s gave 5.80 m/s after 2 s, **3.06 m/s²**, against coasting 12.02→12.61 (R1: 0.05 m/s²). The touch-zone hold took 7.99→4.27 m/s in 1.5 s.
- **Failure 2:** The paddling advantage is inconsistent. Real-time swipes gained +4.39 m/s in 4 s against +2.22 coasting (better than R1). However, `paddle10s` from v=2 covered 61.3 m in one run and 38.3 m in the next, while coasting covered 48.4 m. Paddling *lost* to coasting in one run. Cause: debug paddles in `input.read()` count down with wall-clock `dt`, so `advance()` fires a nondeterministic number of strokes. The tests can therefore neither prove nor regress paddle balance. Auto-calibration at GO works (upright pose now reads 0°, compared with −90° and full lock in R1). One remaining risk: `calibrate()` averages the *next* 10 samples, so a player already turning at GO bakes that turn into zero; the test saw +28.6° tilt read as +6.4°.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/core/input.js`, have the debug stroke cadence take the simulation `dt` (add `read(simDt)` from `fixedUpdate`), and calibrate from the 10 samples *before* GO (a ring buffer), rejecting any sample more than 8° from their median. For the headlight, log `down()` and `up()` roles under `?debug=input`. Also accept `pointerType==='touch'` taps where `up` lands in the same zone even if `role` was reassigned, and confirm that no `.sbd-screen.race` child with `pointer-events:auto` covers the lower-right area.

## 4. Audio & Diegetic Cockpit UI [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The glyphs fade but keep coming back. Measured opacity of `.sbd-glyphs` every 5 s with no touches: `0.34 → 0 (5 s) … 0.02 (25 s) … 0.26 (50 s) … 0 … 0.26 (60 s)`. The fade at GO works (R1: fully opaque at 60 s). However, the 10 s idle flash brings the arcs back at 26 % opacity over the road on a fixed cycle (`glyphs-60s.jpg` shows faint bulb and lever glyphs bottom-right). A coasting player will be nagged by them indefinitely.
- **Failure 2:** The finish now has strong diegetic content (`finish-arch.jpg`: FINISH arch with checker bands, flagman waving a checkered flag, hay-bale chicane, bunting, crowd). But the crowd are capsule dolls with flat-colour bodies and sphere heads, and the hay bales are faceted untextured cylinders. Next to the improved weathered wood they look like placeholders. Audio now uses a modelled foley bank (`src/audio/bank.js`: modal plank synthesis, rendered once), but there are still no recorded samples. I did not verify it by ear in a headless run, so it is not scored up. The results card (`finish.jpg`) lists every AI as **DNF** the moment the player crosses the line, even though the AIs were still racing. It should keep running times or show live gaps.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ui/index.js` `idleFlash()`, flash only before the first paddle stroke of the race and never after 20 s of race time. Gate it on `game.state.raceTime < 20 && !anyStroke`. In `src/scene/scenery.js`, give the hay bales the `straw` texture recipe (end-on spiral normal map, `textures.js`) and replace the crowd capsules with 3–4 instanced low-poly figures (jacket, trousers, hat) with idle sway and arm-wave on pass.

## 6. Graphics, Lighting & PBR Shaders [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The quality tiers are real but still very expensive. In SwiftShader at 1180×820, q=low runs at 1.0 FPS, q=medium at 0.4 and q=default at 0.2, with the chase cam at 1.4. Each tier is about 2× apart, which is a correct ordering, unlike R1 where low was the slowest. But even low doesn't reach 1 fps on CPU raster, which points to a CPU-side cost (scene traversal, draw calls from about 1,000 grass cards) that GPU tiers can't fix. Mud is now genuinely improved (`hazard-mud.jpg`: wet ruts with standing water reflecting the sky, parallax depth). The new gravel (`hazard-gravel.jpg`) tiles so regularly that it reads as a *cobbled street*, not loose farm gravel.
- **Failure 2:** The lens splatter is overdone and flat. `hazard-mud-after.jpg` shows about 18 identical-looking brown discs with soft halos scattered over the upper sky (y < 300 px), where wheel spray could not plausibly reach the eye line. They have no refraction streaks and no drips. The night bloom fix works (`night.jpg`: the "3" flag is legible, R1's was blown out to white) and the dusk stars are gone (`cockpit-dusk-headlight.jpg`).
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/graphics/postfx.js` `addSplatter()`, spawn mud drops with y ∈ [0.45, 1.0] (lower half, weighted toward the bottom), cap them at 8 per hit, and vary the shape with 3 noise octaves plus an elongation along the screen-space velocity. Make the halo alpha 0 and add a 0.3 refraction offset at the rim. In `src/graphics/textures.js`, gravel recipe: break the tiling with a 2-scale stochastic (hex-tile) sample and reduce stone density by 50 %, with dirt showing through. In `src/scene/scenery.js`, merge the grass cards per 50 m chunk into one `InstancedMesh` to cut draw calls.

## 7. The 5 Tiered AI Agents [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Stalls are reduced but not gone. `node tests/ai-sim.mjs 3` (real physics) logged STALL events in 10 of 15 driver-runs, including Ace (`STALL4s@1109`, `STALL3s@732`) and Clementine (`STALL4s@282`). Off-road time stays high for the top tiers: Maeve averaged 19.1 s and Ace 11.1 s. In the 90 s in-game race there was no long stall (R1: Buster stuck for 40 s), but there were transient drops (Barnaby 0.8 m/s at t=40, Maeve 1.9 m/s at t=40, Barnaby 0.1 m/s at t=90).
- **Failure 2:** The tier order holds on average (Ace 291.1 < Maeve 301.9 < Buster 332.0 < Clementine 363.8 < Barnaby 368.0 s, which fixes R1's Buster/Maeve inversion). But Barnaby and Clementine are only 4.2 s apart, and in seed 1 Clementine (388.9 s) was slower than Barnaby (373.2 s). In the in-game race the lead changed hands early (Barnaby t=10, Maeve t=20–30, Ace from t=40), and then Ace pulled 191 m clear by t=90 (s=1185 vs 994). That leaves no pack racing for the second half.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ai/driver.js`, trigger stuck recovery at 1.0 s (not 2 s) when `speed < 2 && |lateral| > hw`, and steer the recovery toward the centreline target, not opposite to the current steer. In `src/ai/planner.js`, add an off-road cost of 25 per metre beyond hw for tiers ≥ 4. In `src/ai/profiles.js`, set Clementine `speed.cap` to 15 (from her current value) and Barnaby `paddle.cadence` ×0.8 to restore the gap. Add a soft rubber band (`draft.strength` 0.15 for drivers more than 80 m behind the leader).
