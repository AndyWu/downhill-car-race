## 1. Scene & Environment (2-Mile Country Dirt Road) [ Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The fix for containment created a new visual problem. Steered drift is now held: max beyond edge **1.63 m** (s=600, steer 0.35), 1.53 m (s=1500), **1.71 m** (s=2200, steer 0.5, 10 m/s), against R4's 15.92/19.58 m. No-input runs peak at 1.27 m. The cost is visible: the new "taller verge lip" renders as a uniform, near-vertical grass *wall* 3–4 m high with stretched, smeared texture on the right side of `dry-road-lens.jpg` and `cockpit-midtrack-speed.jpg`. It has no soil break, no exposed roots, no drainage cut. A country lane bank in TLOU shows layered earth, erosion runnels and overhanging growth; this reads as a hedge-maze wall.
- **Failure 2:** The start zone is still over budget. `renderer.info` (q=default, ai=5, shadows included) at **s=0 was 179–180 calls / 286–287k tris** across two runs, down 19 % from R4's 221/360k but still above the R3/R4 target of ≤ 120 / 200k. Mid-course numbers vary with AI proximity: s=1500 was 91 / 136,864 in one run and **148 / 224,412** in the next (AI trucks inside 33 m), so far-LOD only works when the pack is spread. The flat-section stall is gone: a no-input coast through s=560–620 kept ≥ **2.35 m/s** and exited at 5.72 m/s (R4: parked at 0.1–1.3 m/s for 40 s). In the 90 s race the no-input player had only 3 slow-seconds, but it spent 44 s off-road riding the lip at lateral ≈ ±4.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/scene/ground.js`, split the verge into a 0.6 m soil lip (vertex-coloured exposed earth, `materials.dirtRoad` with darker AO) plus a 30° grass bank above it. Apply triplanar UVs (`worldUV.js`) to the bank so the grass texture doesn't stretch vertically, and scatter 1–2 erosion-runnel decals per 40 m. In `src/scene/scenery.js`, merge the start-zone bales, crowd and bunting into `InstancedMesh` (3 draws), and make `world.update` force AI trucks to far-LOD beyond 20 m while s < 150.

## 2. Network, Offline PWA & QR-WebRTC Star Topology [Opus 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Prediction still has visible error in the own-car state. Stroke loss is fixed: under 15 % loss + 20–80 ms jitter the client "made 123, host applied 123" paddle strokes, and covered **86.6 m vs 87.6 m clean (98.9 %)** (R4: 28 m vs 139 m). The per-frame visual pop is clamped at **2.50 cm** max (R4: 19.34 cm p95). But the *pending* correction reached **115.3 cm** (R4: 62.7 cm), and own-car prediction error was p95 21.7 cm, max 43.9 cm. The clamp hides the error by bleeding it out over about 46 frames, so under loss the client's car visibly drifts back toward truth for nearly a second after each burst. The jitter buffer still logs "lost 60, strokes recovered 0", which is fine now because of cumulative counters, but repeated/merged counts (107/60) show heavy input duplication.
- **Failure 2:** The bandwidth and offline stories are complete, with one gap. In the real game a client uploads **6,680 B / 10.01 s ≈ 667 B/s** and the host sends 2,535 B/s per client at **14.1 B/car**. Loopback and `net-offline.mjs` on the real dist pass (offline reload and deep link, 0 failed requests). What remains untested is a mid-race *disconnect and rejoin*. Nothing in `net-race`/`net-loopback` drops the DataChannel during GO and checks that the client resumes or the host frees the slot, which is the most likely real-world failure on an iPad hotspot (screen lock, Wi-Fi roam).
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/net/sync.js`, cap the largest pending correction by blending the predicted state toward the server state with `α = 1 − exp(−dt/0.08)` *in addition to* the 5 cm/step clamp, and log max pending in the net-race output with an assertion < 50 cm. In `tests/net-race.mjs` (Network-owned), add a stage that calls `pc.close()` on the client at t=6 s, reconnects via a fresh code, and asserts that the host keeps simulating the slot as AI-ghost within 2 s and hands it back on rejoin.

## 3. Core Gameplay, Controls & Flag Physics [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The touch handbrake still doesn't match the debug handbrake. Debug handbrake=1 measures **2.96 m/s²** (11.9→~6 m/s in 2 s). Holding the touch handbrake zone took **10.61 → 0.02 m/s in 1.5 s** of wall time, ≈ 7 m/s² (R4: 11.29 → 0.02). That's the same discrepancy as R4. Caveat: the touch run is real-time in SwiftShader, so sim time may exceed 1.5 s; even so, a full stop from 10.6 m/s is not what a 3 m/s² wooden brake block delivers, so the touch path (drag mapping, or reverse engaging) still adds braking.
- **Failure 2:** Coast/paddle balance improved and controls are solid. On a deterministic `norender` run, a 10 s coast covered **28.34 m (×2 identical)** against paddling **74.03 m**, a 2.6× ratio (R4: 3.6×). The headlight goes on→off→on (`[true,false,true]`) and tilt is symmetric (±28.6° → ±0.61). The remaining feel gap is the first 10 s: the no-input player was at s=68 at 4.8 m/s at t=10, while paddling AIs were at 66–80 m. The start ramp alone gives a coaster near-AI pace, so paddling technique barely matters off the line.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/core/input.js`, when `p.role==='handbrake'`, clamp `S.touchHB = clamp(…, 0, 1)` and ignore `gear` role changes for any pointer that started in the handbrake zone. In `src/core/physics.js`, cap handbrake force at `brakeForce·handbrake` with no extra term from `reverse`. Add a `tests/core-r4.mjs` case driving the handbrake through synthetic pointer events with `advance()` (sim time) and asserting decel within 15 % of the debug path. Lower the start-ramp exit speed (`scene/track.js` ramp grade) by about 20 % so paddling decides the first 100 m.

## 4. Audio & Diegetic Cockpit UI [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Results-card projections are now ordered correctly but the times are wrong. At the player's finish (1:37.65 after a teleport to s=3150), the card lists "Maeve ~537 s, Ace ~551 s, Buster ~551 s, Clementine ~572 s, Barnaby ~583 s". The order matches actual positions at t=90 (Maeve 963 > Buster 912 ≈ Ace 911 > Clementine 824 > Barnaby 789). But the headless sim's full-race averages for the same drivers are **274.7–360.9 s**, so the projection overestimates by about 1.6–1.9×. The card tells a family "Maeve will take 9 minutes" for a 4.5-minute race.
- **Failure 2:** Audio and glyphs are fixed. `tests/audio-metrics.mjs` now asserts and passes: sand centroid **1,223 Hz** (R3: 4,137), gravel 1,992 Hz, mud 501 Hz with 73 % < 250 Hz, ambient **−26.5 dBFS** (R3: −35.5) with a low-weighted 527 Hz centroid. Glyph opacity was 0.33 at t=0 and **0 at every sample from 5 s to 60 s** (R4 flashed 0.26 at 15–20 s). What remains is that the ambient bed is a static spectrum. There is no evidence of positional events (birds passing, a tractor across a field, crowd swelling at the finish), and that is where TLOU's soundscape does its storytelling. The finish crowd and bunting are visually present but not heard as a crowd in the metrics.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ui/index.js`, project as `raceTime + (TRACK_LENGTH − s) / v̄`, where `v̄` is the vehicle's *mean* speed since GO (`s / raceTime`) blended 50/50 with its 20 s average. Compare against `ai-sim` in a `tests/ui-projection.mjs` assertion (±15 %). In `src/audio/index.js`, add positional one-shots: a crowd loop anchored at the finish arch (gain ∝ 1/distance from s=3050) and 2–3 PannerNode bird calls placed off-road.

## 5. The 10 Homemade Soap Box Trucks [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The R4 decal defects are fixed but the trucks are still toy-clean from behind. Rear plates are single and crisp (`trucks-contact-sheet.jpg`: "13", "21" with flame stencil, "42", "10"), and the barrel/flag numbers agree (truck 3: barrel "5", cockpit flag "5" in `cockpit-midtrack-speed.jpg`). But the ten rears in the contact sheet carry no road grime, no chipped paint and no dust gradient toward the wheels. Their paint is uniform and saturated (the yellow "42" board, the blue "10" crate) next to a dirt road that should be coating them.
- **Failure 2:** Mud on cockpit parts now reads as mud, but it's coarse. After mud, the wheel rim and paddles take a brown wet coat (`mud-splatter-coarse-steps.jpg`: rim visibly browned with a glossy sheen; `hazard-mud-after.jpg`: paddle faces streaked). The lever knob tops stay clean red/brown, and there is no visible wet-to-dry transition because no screenshot pair spans the 4 s dry window. The coat is a uniform tint over the whole part rather than splatter concentrated on surfaces facing the front wheels.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/vehicles/kit.js`, add a per-truck `roadGrime` layer (world-space Y-gradient: 0.6 dirt tint below axle height fading to 0 at 0.6 m) applied to every body material from the start, and modulate saturation by −25 % at spawn. Have `mudCoat` sample a directional mask (dot of the part's normal with the forward/down vector) so faces toward the wheels get 2× coat. Extend `tests/trucks-preview.mjs` to screenshot the cockpit at t=0.5 s and t=5 s after mud and assert a roughness change > 0.3.

## 6. Graphics, Lighting & PBR Shaders [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** Dry-road water drops still appear. After the mud runs, teleporting to s=1500 (dry dirt) and rendering 60 frames at 1/30 s (`dry-road-lens.jpg`) showed **5 clear refracting water drops** on the lens, one large one over the radiator cap. `cockpit-midtrack-speed.jpg` (fresh load, teleport s=1500, dry) shows one more. The unit test `tests/splatter-sim.mjs` passes all checks ("no drops without contact", "water ignored with wetSteps = 1", "clear() empties drops"), so the defect is in integration: `teleport()` clears and a 1.5 s dry run still has drops, which means something in-game feeds water contact on dry road (rutpool hazards near the line, or the wet latch surviving the teleport).
- **Failure 2:** Lens mud is now frame-rate independent but looks the same every time. Fine-step (1/30 s, `mud-splatter-rendered.jpg`) and coarse-step (0.25 s, `mud-splatter-coarse-steps.jpg`) runs both show about 8–10 drops, matching `splatter-sim`'s "spawned counts match across frame rates: 21/21/21" (R4: zero vs ten). But every drop is the same translucent brown teardrop with an **identical centred white specular dot**, several sit at mid-screen height (y≈380–460), and none has a streak or drip trail despite 9–12 m/s speed. FPS in SwiftShader: q-low 1.8, chase 1.8 (R4: 2.0 / 2.0), with no further gain.
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/main.js`, call `post.clearSplatter()` *and* reset the physics wet latch (`body.state.wetSteps = 0`) inside `teleportVehicle`. In `src/graphics/postfx.js`, spawn water drops only from `surface==='water'|'puddle'|'stream'`, never 'rutpool' under 0.3 m depth. Give each mud drop a random specular offset (±0.3 r) and size (0.4–1.0), elongate it along screen-space velocity by `1 + speed/8`, and add a 3-tap drip trail when age > 1 s. Weight spawn y toward the lower 40 % (y ∈ [0.55, 1]).

## 7. The 5 Tiered AI Agents [Sonnet 5.5 Medium]
- **Failure 1:** The in-game order collapsed at the top. The 90 s ai=5 race finished **Maeve 963 > Buster 912 > Ace 911 > Clementine 824 > Barnaby 789**: the tier-5 Pro is third, and tied with tier 3. `tests/ai-game.mjs` agrees: `Maeve 959 > Buster 908 > Ace 907 > Clementine 820 > Barnaby 785`. Ace dropped to 5.3 m/s at t=20 (s=145, likely the new "proximity give-room" yielding), and the new "leader ease" then caps whoever leads. The fixes for Maeve worked against the tier the game is named for. Only **2 AI lead changes** occurred in 90 s.
- **Failure 2:** The headless sim fails its own new gates. `node tests/ai-sim.mjs 3` printed **FAIL: fewer than 4 lead changes per race on average** (first 90 s avg 2.0, whole race 2.3; the commit message claims 4.8) and **FAIL: Maeve ahead of Buster at 90 s in too few seeds** (2/3). Averages still order correctly (Ace 274.7 < Maeve 303.3 < Buster 334.7 < Clementine 346.0 < Barnaby 360.9). But in seed 1 Buster leads Maeve at 90 s (887 vs 827), and seed 3 is effectively a tie (803 vs 801). Stalls are gone (slow-seconds ≤ 1 for all AIs in-game, off-road ≤ 2 s).
- **Concrete Fix:** In `src/ai/driver.js`, exempt tier ≥ 5 from "give-room" yielding, and make "leader ease" apply only when the leader is more than 60 m ahead *and* past s=1200, so Ace isn't throttled in the opening. In `src/ai/profiles.js`, give Ace `paddle.strength` ×1.06 to offset any signature-truck drag. Add to `tests/ai-game.mjs` an assertion that Ace is top 2 at t=90 and that there are ≥ 3 lead changes. Then reconcile the commit's "4.8 lead changes" against `ai-sim`'s measured 2.0, since the claim doesn't reproduce.
