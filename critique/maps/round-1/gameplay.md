# GAMEPLAY CRITIQUE — MAP ROUND 1
**Score:** village 6/10, corn 6/10, circuit 5/10

How I tested: `node tests/ai-sim.mjs 3 --track=<id>` and `node tests/ai-game.mjs --track=<id>` for all three maps, with country as the reference. I also ran a scratch Playwright harness against my own build (`vite build` into scratch and `vite preview`). It drove the built game through `__game.advance()` with `ai=9`. It ran:
- a full 10-car race, with the player on an autopilot that follows the road and paddles below a target speed;
- 16 "bad driving" runs per map (4 s of full steering lock while paddling, into each side, at 8 points along the map), timing how long recovery took;
- 2 "wedge" runs per map (steer into the side and stop paddling);
- `renderer.info` draw calls and triangles at 6 points per map (`q=medium`, 960×540, pack around the player);
- a touch layout check at 844×390.

The raw numbers are in `screens/gameplay/<map>-results.json`.

Reference numbers (same harness and autopilot):

## What works (max 3 bullets)
- **Containment is solid on all three maps.** Over 48 full-lock bad-driving runs, the cart never left the play area. Village walls hold it at |lat| ≤ 4.2 m and corn walls at ≤ 3.5 m. On circuit the cart stops on the run-off and barrier (|lat| ≈ 8 m). Recovery back to the road at speed took a median of about 0.7 s. On corn it never took more than 1.1 s, and only 2 of 48 runs took more than 3.5 s (village, see below). Solo good-driver runs had no rescues and no off-road time on any map.
- **The UI works.** The rank shows the live place, and the circuit lap chip counts "Lap 1/3 → 3/3" and is hidden on the one-lap maps. The race map draws the circuit as a closed loop with a start line, and the village and corn maps as a winding line with dots. At 844×390 the paddle boxes and the map sit side by side without touching: the right paddle box ends at x=720 and the map starts at x=763. Evidence: `village-phone-844x390.jpg`, `corn-phone-844x390.jpg`, `circuit-phone-844x390.jpg`.
- **Frame cost is fine for an iPad.** With ai=9, all points measured 57–144 draw calls and 80k–270k triangles. The heaviest were village s=644 in the market split (144 calls, 238k triangles), corn s=644 (144 calls, 234k), and circuit at the grid with the grandstands (128 calls, 269k). Physics plus a CPU frame in swiftshader took 3–15 ms.

## Problems (ranked, most severe first; each: what / where (s metres, street) / evidence file / why it matters)
1. **[circuit] A cart wedged against the run-off or barrier is never rescued.** `flags.noStuckRescue: true` turns off the 5 s stuck rescue. In the wedge test at s=644 (steer into the left run-off, stop paddling), the cart sat at 0.01 m/s for more than 20 s and nothing happened. The other wedge test, at s=322, was rescued after 4.3 s, but that came from the wrong-way rescue, not the stuck rescue. A child who drives nose-first into the tyre wall has to discover the reverse lever on their own. Evidence: `circuit-wedge-644.jpg`, `circuit-results.json` → `bad.wedge`.
2. **[circuit] The race is far too long and spreads out until carts get lapped.** Three laps of 1073 m on the flat, driven only by paddling, take the best AI 353–395 s and the last AI 529–627 s. That is 6–10 minutes of continuous paddling on an iPad, against country's 4.6–6.4 minutes downhill. At 90 s in the game, Ace is already 377 m (a third of a lap) ahead of Barnaby. At 420 s, 6 of 9 AI were still running (Barnaby at 2656/3218). Skill order is shaky: ρ 0.78, and seed 3 gave 0.52 with Buster and Rosco at 627 s. Evidence: sim output, `circuit-race-end.jpg`, `circuit-results.json` → `race`.
3. **[corn] The race is a procession on a single-file lane: no lead changes, and stuck AI pile up behind slow carts.** The sim gives 0.0 lead changes per race, with Ace winning wire to wire at 320.2 s in every seed. In the game, skilled AI got stuck behind weaker ones for the whole race. The patient-on-narrow-lane rule (`driver.js`, `plan.narrow` caps `vt` at the car ahead + 0.4) held Maeve (skill 0.73, 347 s in the sim) behind Dot and Rosco: s=574/575 at 75 s, and still bunched at 2402–2411 at 330 s. At 420 s, 6 of 9 AI had not finished. AI rescues clustered where these trains bunched up: s≈1542–1562 and s≈2249–2342 (Dot, Clementine, Tilly). Evidence: `corn-stuck-1545.jpg`, `corn-stuck-2330.jpg`, `corn-results.json`.
4. **[village, corn, circuit] A decent player is never challenged and races alone from the start.** The player starts on pole (slot 0 is the front row on every map). The AI also has a speed cap on these maps: `TRACK_CAP` in `src/ai/driver.js` (village 7.4+4.1k m/s, corn 7.0+3.9k m/s), plus the flat-cadence cap on circuit. A road-following autopilot was 1st/10 at every 30 s check on all three maps. Its finishing margin over Ace was 80 s (village), 90 s (corn) and 108 s (circuit), against 34 s on country. On corn the player shows rank 1/10 after 12 s and never sees an opponent again. Even a relaxed driver who paddles 60 % of the time beat Ace on village (288 vs 296) and corn (303 vs 319). Evidence: `*-results.json` → `race.ranks`, `corn-phone-844x390.jpg` (1/10 at 12 s).
5. **[village] Too many AI stalls and rescues, and the best AI lose time at junction mouths.** The sim averaged about 12 rescues per race (country: about 3.7), with Vance at 9, Clementine 8 and Dot 6. ρ is 0.76 (FAIL < 0.8) and ρ at 90 s is 0.61. In `ai-game`, Vance (skill 0.84) was 8th of 9 at 90 s (624 m). In the in-game race, the top three AI (Ace, Maeve, Vance) were all stuck for 3–3.5 s and then rescued at s≈1508–1536, on Main Street 60–85 m into split 2. Stall props sit at the kerb there. Other rescue spots: s≈149–159 (Barnaby and Tilly at the first hazard, 27 s in), s≈1693 (split 2 exit) and s≈2469–2493 (Buster, split 3 Riverside entry). Evidence: `village-stuck-1525.jpg`, `village-stuck-150.jpg`, `village-stuck-2475.jpg`, `village-results.json` → `race.rescues` / `stuckEv`.
6. **[village] Two of 16 bad-driving runs took a long time to recover.** At s=644 (market split), a cart steered left stopped at 0.5 m/s and needed a respawn, 8.9 s in total. At s=2639 (split 3), a cart steered left took 11.1 s. Every other run recovered in under 2.6 s. In the wedge test, village rescued after 7.4–8.7 s and corn after 9.9–10.8 s, which is long for a child sitting still. Evidence: `village-results.json` → `bad.res`.
7. **[all maps] Finished AI carts keep getting "rescued" every 2.5 s.** In `fixedUpdate` (`src/main.js`), `if (v.ai.needsRescue) v.body.recover(8)` runs before the `v.finished` check, so a parked finished AI counts as stuck. The in-game races logged 90 (corn) and 42 (circuit) post-finish respawns at s=3216. For the first 5 s, while the cart is still visible, it hops forward along the finish straight. It also wastes physics and AI work.
8. **[circuit] The lap chip overlaps the left paddle box at 844×390.** `.sbd-lap` (13,61 62×25) overlaps `.sbd-padbox.l`, which starts at y=66, by about 50×20 px. This is cosmetic (the pad box has `pointer-events:none`), but the chip sits on the dashed box outline. Evidence: `circuit-phone-844x390.jpg`.
9. **[all maps, minor] GPU memory grows on each restart.** Each `startRace` on the same track adds about 28 geometries and 10 textures (village, 4 restarts: 143/47 → 237/77), so a long rematch session keeps growing. `clearRace` removes vehicle groups but does not dispose them.

## Fix list (concrete, actionable, ordered; name files/functions where you can)
1. **Circuit stuck rescue** (`src/scene/tracks/circuit.js` flags, or `src/core/physics.js` `step()`): keep the stuck rescue on circuit, but only fire it when the cart is off the asphalt (|lateral| > hw) or touching a wall surface. Use about 4 s off-road/wall, and don't fire while the player is simply not paddling on the track. Alternatively, drop `noStuckRescue` and use a longer timer (8 s) when on the track.
2. **Shorten the circuit race**: cut it to 2 laps, or a shorter lap of about 700–800 m, so the best AI finishes in about 4 minutes and the last in under 5:30. Also narrow the AI spread on the flat: in `src/ai/driver.js`, `flatCad` and `leaderEase` should be stronger when `flat`, so nobody is lapped. Target sim ρ ≥ 0.8 on every seed.
3. **Make the AI a real opponent on the new maps** (`src/ai/driver.js` `TRACK_CAP`): raise the caps so Ace on village and corn runs within about 30–40 s of a good human, as on country (for example village 8.5+5.5k, corn 8.0+5.2k), then re-check with ai-sim that ρ stays ≥ 0.8. On narrow or single-file maps (corn, village alleys), consider starting the player mid-grid instead of on pole. That means `aiSlot()`/startRace slot mapping for corn, village and circuit, without changing country.
4. **Corn passing**: add 3–4 passing bays (lane widened to ≥ 6.5 m for 30–40 m) at about s 600, 1300, 2000 and 2700 (`src/scene/tracks/corn.js`). In `driver.js`, have patient drivers pass in the bays (lift the `plan.narrow` `vt` cap where `plan.widthAt(s) > 6`), so faster AI get by and lead changes become possible (target ≥ 1 per race in sim).
5. **Village junction stalls**: move or shrink the kerb props on Main Street at s≈1500–1540 (split 2 mouth) and the first hazard at s≈150 (`src/scene/tracks/village.js` `noStall` and prop placement), or add them to the AI cost so the line avoids them (`planner.js` hazard list). Re-run ai-sim until rescues per race are ≤ 5 and ρ ≥ 0.8. Check the market split at s≈644 and split 3 at s≈2639, where a left-side bad-driving run took 9–11 s to recover.
6. **`src/main.js` `fixedUpdate`**: skip the AI rescue for finished cars (`if (!v.finished && v.ai.needsRescue)`).
7. **`src/ui/styles.js`**: place `.sbd-lap` beside `.sbd-rank` (right of it) or move the pad boxes down about 25 px, so the lap chip doesn't overlap `.sbd-padbox.l` at 844×390.
8. **`src/main.js` `clearRace`**: dispose vehicle geometries, materials and flag textures (traverse `v.group`) to stop the per-restart memory growth.
9. **Stuck rescue speed on corn and village**: with the 5 s stuck timer, a stopped child waits 8–11 s for a rescue. Consider 3.5 s once the speed has been < 0.4 m/s and the cart is in contact with a wall or corn surface.
