## What works (max 3 bullets)
- At cockpit height the lane really is a corn corridor: tall walls, cards with tassels in front, it reads as "narrow lane in a corn field" at once (`lane_cockpit_0000.jpg`, `0450`, `1800`). Night (stars, dark walls) and dusk silhouettes of the windmill/scarecrow look moody (`night_cockpit_790.jpg`, `dusk_cockpit_790.jpg`).
- Containment is solid: steering full-lock into either wall for 3 s at 9–11 m/s never got the cart into the corn (max |lateral| 3.0 m on a ~1.75 m half-width), it slows on the "corn" surface and comes back to the lane once you stop steering; no flips, no stuck cart (drive logs; `drive_cockpit_04_steerR_s559.jpg`, `drive_cockpit_05_after_s608.jpg`).
- The barn yards are nicely dressed (barn, silo, farmhouse, windmill, mailbox, a dirt drive cut up the bank) when you can see them (`far_cam_780.jpg`, `farm_2020_from_lane.jpg`, `aerial_0780_farm.jpg`).

## Problems (ranked, most severe first; each: what / where (s metres, street) / evidence file / why it matters)
1. **No start and no finish at all.** No banner, arch, line, flags, hay-bale grid or crowd at s=0 or s=3218; a scene traverse finds no `start*/finish*/banner*` object. The race just ends in the middle of an identical corn corridor. / s=0, s=3218 / `start_grid.jpg`, `finish_approach.jpg`, `race_finish_approach_chase.jpg`, `far_cam_3190.jpg`, `lane_cockpit_3150.jpg` / every other map (country, circuit) has a finish structure; on a 5-minute lane with no landmarks, the finish is the one payoff and it is missing.
2. **Chase / hood cameras go inside the corn on every bend.** With the cart near the verge (which happens constantly, see 4), the trailing camera sits in or behind the wall: the screen fills with a giant smeared green plane or the dark interior of the field, and the cart is hidden. / s≈220, 540, 620, 700 (any bend) / `drive_chase_01_s219.jpg`, `drive_chase_04_steerR_s542.jpg`, `drive_chase_06_steerL_s624.jpg`, `drive_chase_08_steerR_s704.jpg` / chase is the default view for many kids; half the frames of my chase drive were unreadable.
3. **The clearings are hidden behind a dark 1.5–2 m earth bank.** The verge lip (`verge()` → `lip` 1.35–2 m) is kept in the clearings, so from the lane (eye height ~1.3 m) a pumpkin patch / hay yard / barn yard is a flat dark-green wall with fence rails and the tops of a few pumpkins poking over it. The bank's dark streaky texture also reads as a hedge, not as a field edge. / pumpkin 330–405 R, 1180–1250 R, 2430–2505 L; hay 1620–1700 L, 2820–2890 R; farm 720–840 L / `side_0370_pumpkin.jpg`, `side_1215_pumpkin.jpg`, `side_1660_hay.jpg`, `side_2470_pumpkin.jpg`, `lane_cockpit_1200.jpg`, `lane_cockpit_1650.jpg`, `lane_cockpit_2850.jpg` / the clearings are the map's only variety and its theme (pumpkins, scarecrows), and the driver never sees them.
4. **Pumpkin-patch fence rails are rotated 90°: they stick out across the lane.** Rails (`boxG(4,…)` with `ry = atan2(-rhz, rhx)`) lie along the road's right vector, so every 4 m a 4 m rail juts from the bank-top post over the lane at head height. In cockpit one rail crosses the sky diagonally right in front of you. / every pumpkin clearing (330–405, 1180–1250, 2430–2505) / `drive_cockpit_02_s370.jpg`, `pumpkin_patch_2470_cockpitish.jpg`, `side_1215_pumpkin.jpg`, `aerial_0370_pumpkin.jpg` (the "comb" of perpendicular dashes along the lane) / looks broken, and reads as an obstacle you should hit.
5. **The corn reads as a lawn / green cardboard from anywhere but the lane.** The canopy is a single smooth sheet 2.7 m up, 64 m wide, using a grass-like streaky texture; from the chase/far camera and every elevated view the field is rolling grass hills with a row of cards at the lane edge, and the canopy ends in a visible ledge at 64 m. Close up the wall face is a stretched vertical-streak texture (big blurry planes in `drive_cockpit_04`, `06`). / whole map / `aerial_0200_corridor.jpg`, `aerial_0000_start.jpg`, `wall_behind_0560.jpg`, `far_cam_1215.jpg`, `far_cam_3190.jpg`, `birds_zoom_flock0_elevated.jpg`, `drive_cockpit_06_steerL_s645.jpg` / far view is a regular camera option; the "corn sea" is the map's identity and it currently looks like a grass verge.
6. **Repetition.** 15 of 22 cockpit shots taken every 150 m are the same frame (two walls, brown lane, sky gap); there are no corn-side props (no tractor in a side row, cut-row gaps, signs, crow on a post, irrigation pipes, dropped pumpkins, bales on the verge) and the walls are the same height and hue everywhere. / s 0–300, 450–700, 850–1150, 1300–1600, 1750–1950, 2100–2400, 2550–2800, 2900–3218 / `lane_cockpit_*.jpg` (compare 0000, 0450, 0600, 1350, 1500, 1800, 2100, 2250, 3150) / a 5-minute descent with nothing new for 300 m at a time is boring for kids.
7. **Crows are nearly invisible and only react to the camera.** Flocks circle 9–23 m off the lane, 5–9 m above the canopy; from the lane you see 3–6 grey specks for a second (`birds_f0_approach_06…11.jpg`), perched birds sit on top of the canopy where no one can see them, and the crows render light grey instead of black (`birds_zoom_flock0_elevated.jpg`). `main.js` calls `world.update(dt, camera)` without `actors`, so AI carts never scatter them — only the player camera does. / flocks at s 180, 520, 910, 1300, 1640, 2000, 2350, 2700, 3010 / `birds_f0_approach_*.jpg`, `birds_zoom_flock0_from_lane.jpg` / the "crows burst up as you pass" moment is not readable.
8. **Pumpkins look like potatoes/bread loaves.** Smooth pale-tan icosahedra with no ribs, no stems, no leaves on the vine; colour reads beige-yellow, not orange, under the `dark` material. / all pumpkin patches / `infield_0380_pumpkin_lookback.jpg`, `infield_1220_pumpkin.jpg`, `clearing_edge_1250.jpg`, `far_cam_1215.jpg` / pumpkins are in the map's name.
9. **Lane pull to the verge on gentle bends; strong cart roll on the bank.** Paddling straight with no steering, the cart drifts onto the bank within 200 m and rides it at 25–40° of roll (upY 0.67–0.9), the cockpit view leans hard into the wall. Not a block, but the wall-riding is the normal state for a kid who does not steer precisely on a 3.0–3.8 m lane. / s 200–500 / `drive_cockpit_01_s228.jpg`, `drive_cockpit_04_steerR_s559.jpg`, `drive_chase_*` logs / also feeds problem 2.
10. **Minor:** dusk turns the whole corridor a flat magenta-brown with almost no contrast between lane and wall (`dusk_cockpit_150.jpg`); at night nothing is lit (no lantern at barns / pumpkin stand). In a 10-car race after ~30 s, five AI were bunched at s≈180 doing ~4 m/s (gameplay critic should check for an AI jam in the first bend).

## Fix list (concrete, actionable, ordered; name files/functions where you can)
1. `corn.js` `decorate`: add a start area (hay-bale grid boxes, a farm-gate arch or two poles with a "CORN & PUMPKINS" banner via `bannerTexture`, a checkered line decal) at s≈0–15 and a finish arch + checkered line + a few straw-bale spectator rows/flags at s=LEN (copy the pattern from `circuit.js` ~l.150–165 / `ground.js` FINISH block). Keep it outside the lane clearance and register posts with `reg.circle`.
2. `corn.js` `verge`: when `inClearing(s, side)` (need it per side → pass `d`'s sign), drop the lip to ≤0.35 m (keep the foot ramp for containment) so the patches are visible from the lane; replace the dark streaky bank colour in clearings with the normal field colour (`theme.terrColor`). If containment needs the lip, use a low split-rail fence instead (posts registered as obstacles).
3. `corn.js` pumpkin fence: rotate rails to follow the lane: in `cx()` use `ry: Math.atan2(P.rhx, P.rhz)` for the rails (= current value + π/2, so the box's local x lies along the tangent; keep the old `ry` for the sign board), and put posts at `hw + 1.6` so they sit at the bank foot, not on its crest.
4. Camera vs. corn (coordinate with gameplay fixer, `src/core/camera.js`): add a lateral clamp for chase/hood views — keep the camera within `|lateral| ≤ hw + 0.8` of the lane (a `world.track.project` + pull-in), or let the corn wall fade/discard when the camera is closer than ~1.5 m (`cornwall` material: dither by camera distance). Also consider widening the lane slightly at bends (`widthFn`: `+0.4*min(1,|kap|/0.03)` instead of `-0.4`) so carts and cameras have room on the bends.
5. Corn from above: give the canopy sheet a real corn-top texture (tassel/leaf tips, darker gaps between rows, row striping aligned to the lane) and a jagged top edge; add a sparse instanced layer of cards on the canopy (every ~3 m, 2–3 rows) out to 15–20 m so chase/far views see corn; close the outer edge at 64 m with a down-skirt so there is no ledge.
6. Wall face: tile the `cornwall` texture at a smaller vertical scale and add a second, darker layer of cards 0.4 m behind the front row so the face has depth; vary `CORN_H` ±15% in 30–60 m stretches (taller/shorter, greener/yellower) to break repetition.
7. Variety along the corridor: add 1 small feature every ~120–150 m between clearings: a side-row gap with a parked tractor/wagon, a crow on a fence post, a hand-painted "PUMPKINS ↓ 200 m" sign before each patch, a stray pumpkin or bale on the verge (outside the drive line), a cut corner where you see over the field.
8. Crows: make them black (dedicated material, not `dark` with ~0.07 instance colour that ends up grey), 1.5–2x larger, circle closer (off `hw + 4…10`, height 3–5 m above canopy), perch some on the fence posts / scarecrows / barn roof where they are visible; pass `actors` from `main.js` (`world.update(dt, camera, game.vehicles.map(v => v.body.state.position))`) so AI carts scatter them too (gameplay fixer owns main.js).
9. Pumpkins: use a ribbed geometry (lathe or squashed sphere with 8 radial dents), add a short green-brown stem, saturate colour to (0.95, 0.42, 0.05) and use a material that does not wash it out; add vine/leaf decals between them.
10. Lighting: give the dusk preset more separation for this map (lighter lane colour or a warm rim on the walls), and add 2–3 lanterns/porch lights at each barn yard and the finish for night.
