## What works (max 3 bullets)
- The street canyon itself has real charm at cockpit height: varied plaster/brick facades, shutters, flower boxes, awnings, chimneys, a long winding downhill vista (`main-0250.jpg`, `house-side-oblique.jpg`). The church tower + spire is a good landmark (`church-far.jpg`).
- Fully drivable: every street and junction was driven without getting stuck, hitting a wall or a prop; prop clearance/grounding checks pass; speeds stay 10–13 m/s.
- Rain reads well — darker wet cobbles, drops on the lens, moody street (`rain-chase-split1.jpg`, `rain-chase-river.jpg`).

## Problems (ranked, most severe first)
1. **Branch streets have no identity — "Riverside Lane" has no river at all.** Both Riverside Lanes (split 2 path 1, split 3 path 2) are identical to Main Street: houses both sides, no water, bank, railings, quay or bridge (`br2-1-river-s1550.jpg`, `br2-1-river-s1670.jpg`, `br3-2-river-s2504.jpg`, `aerial-river2.jpg`). Market Square is just a wider street lined with long blank grey garden walls and a few stalls — no square/plaza shape, no fountain/well, no market crowd (`br1-1-market-s656.jpg`, `aerial-market1.jpg`). Back Alleys are mostly a grey concrete trench between 3 m blank walls (`br2-2-alley-s1488.jpg`, `br2-2-alley-s1670.jpg`, `rain-chase-alley.jpg`). The whole point of the map (choose a street) is invisible; the player cannot tell which street they are on.
2. **No start and no finish.** The grid sits in an empty meadow in a walled cobble trench with no gate/banner/crowd (`start-far.jpg`, `fog-dusk-start-grid.jpg`); the finish (s 3218) has no line, arch or banner — the village just stops and the walled trench runs on into a field (`main-3200-finish.jpg`, `finish-far.jpg`, `finish-beyond.jpg`). `village.js` has a `finish` landmark but builds nothing.
3. **Junction geometry is ugly at all 6 junctions.** Where the verge berms of two roads intersect they form sharp cobble "pyramids"/sawtooth mounds and torn texture seams right in the mouth of the junction (`main-0730.jpg`, `main-1450.jpg`, `main-1690.jpg`, `junc2-merge-from1-cockpit.jpg`, `junc2-merge-from2-cockpit.jpg`, `drive2-alley-03.jpg`). From the cockpit 8 m before a split you cannot see that there are 3 choices and there are no signposts (`junc1-split-cockpit.jpg`). Gore wedges between diverging streets are bare grass (`junc2-merge-top.jpg`, `junc3-merge-top.jpg`). In the split-1 alley the street-wall mesh has missing segments that leave a floating V-shaped wall piece (`rain-chase-alley.jpg`).
4. **Hazard props sit on cobble cones / clip through cars.** Every stall/rock hazard lifts the terrain into a sharp cobble-textured pyramid; crate stacks and stalls sit on (or sink into) these cones (`main-stall-347.jpg`, `br1-1-market-s596.jpg`, `br1-1-market-s711.jpg`). "Rock" hazards are only those cobble cones — no actual rock/rubble. Cart shafts (1.8 m poles at x=-1.9 in the cart builder) stick far outside the hazard box and pass straight through the player's cart (`drive3-market-04.jpg`, `br3-1-market-s2569.jpg`).
5. **Street edge reads as a half-pipe, not a kerb.** The 2.1 m verge wall is terrain textured with the same cobble pattern sheared up a slope, giving a repeated diagonal "chevron" band along all 3.2 km and a skate-ramp look in chase view (`main-0010.jpg`, `drive1-market-00.jpg`, `drive2-alley-00.jpg`). No pavement, no vertical kerb stone, doors float at the top of the berm.
6. **One-house-deep film set.** From any raised or chase view the village is a single ribbon of houses with open meadow directly behind and between them; gaps are filled with 3.1 m blank grey garden walls and windowless gable walls (`junc1-split-far.jpg`, `junc1-split-top.jpg`, `aerial-market1.jpg`, `behind-houses.jpg`, `main-0850.jpg`, `main-2050.jpg`). No back rows/rooftops, so it never feels like a hill village.
7. **Empty and lifeless.** No people, spectators, cats/dogs, carts in motion, benches, signs or well; washing lines in alleys are coloured rectangles floating with no rope (`alley-lookup-washing.jpg`); lamps and windows do not light up at dusk and fog hides everything past ~40 m (`fog-dusk-church.jpg`).
8. **Race map can't show the streets.** Branches are drawn but at this scale (3.2 km on a ~300 px strip) the 16–40 m offsets are invisible; the map looks like one line (`rain-alley2-hud-map.jpg`).
9. **Route balance is backwards to its description.** The Back Alley, labelled "Shortest", is the slowest path at both splits (+0.75–2.25 s); differences between all routes are ≤ 2.25 s, so choice barely matters.
10. Minor: dashed dark seam lines across many plaster gable walls (box top vs. gable prism) (`main-0130.jpg`, `main-1330.jpg`, `drive3-market-04.jpg`); cobble texture is ~1 m slabs that read as paving rather than cobbles and is the same grey for the whole map; daytime haze whitens everything beyond ~120 m; distant tree LODs are pale yellow-white blobs (`aerial-market1.jpg`, `junc1-split-far.jpg`).

## Fix list (concrete, actionable, ordered)
1. **Give each branch its own dressing in `village.js` `decorate()`** (switch on `bd.road.kind`):
   - `river`: on one side replace the frontage with a quay — a 2–3 m drop to an animated water strip (reuse the water material/hazards' water look), stone parapet or iron railing along the road edge (registry `poly('rail', …)`), a small footbridge crossing it, willows; only houses on the other side. Keep the `verge`/contain wall but make it a low parapet there.
   - `market`: widen to a real square in the middle third (pad the frontage back 6–10 m from the road on both sides via `ROW`), paving in a lighter/patterned colour (`theme.roadColor` by `road.kind`), central well or the fountain, more stalls along the edges with goods, crates, barrels, a few static villager figures and benches; no blank garden walls in the market (skip the garden-wall fallback for `market`).
   - `alley`: houses tight on both sides instead of garden walls (lower `w`, allow narrower houses), steps visible as real stone treads, washing lines with a rope (thin dark quad between the two anchor points), bins/barrels in door niches, arch over the alley entrance.
2. **Build a start and finish** in `decorate()`: a start ramp/gate with "START" banner, bunting and a small crowd at s≈0–15 (and houses/church square around it instead of open field); a "FINISH" banner arch with chequered line painted on the cobbles at `LEN` (use `bannerTexture` like `circuit.js`), plus a crowd/stand. Extend `frontage()` to `-PRE` and `LEN + POST` so the street does not end in a meadow, or end the run-out in a village square/plaza with buildings closing the view.
3. **Clean up junctions**: in `verge()` (village.js) or terrainnet, take the *min* of the verge heights of overlapping roads near a junction (or flatten verge to 0 where `reg.clear` from a second road < OUT) so berms don't intersect into pyramids; fill the gore wedge with a building or a wall-enclosed garden + a fingerpost sign naming both streets ("Market Square ←  → Back Alley") 25 m before each split; fix the missing street-wall segments (`rowOK` gaps) by ending the wall cleanly instead of leaving a floating triangle.
4. **Hazard props**: don't lift terrain under stalls/crates on visuals (or set `lift` so the prop base sits flush and hide the cone with a flat footprint); replace "rock" cones with actual rubble/loose cobbles/a fallen barrel; shorten cart shafts to stay within the `reg.box('hazard', M, 0, 0, 1.15, 1.05 …)` footprint (or enlarge the box to include them).
5. **Street edge**: make the verge a vertical kerb (0.15 m) + a 1–1.5 m pavement + vertical plinth wall, with its own stone/plaster colour instead of the sheared road texture (`theme.terrColor` for `off < 6.5`, and steeper `verge` smoothstep); stagger door steps onto the pavement.
6. **Depth**: add a second (and third, cheaper) row of roofs behind the frontage (simple boxes + roofs, no windows needed, far LOD), fill gaps between houses with narrower houses or archways instead of 3 m blank walls, add windows to street-facing side walls; terrace the hill behind with garden walls/orchards so the village climbs the slope.
7. **Life**: static villagers waving at the start/finish/market, a few cats/dogs, flower pots, benches, signboards; emissive lamp heads + warm lit windows for `dusk`/`night` (materials with `emissive`, toggled from `world.setWeather`/time).
8. **Race map** (`src/ui` map, or `world.mapPaths`): draw branches in a distinct colour and widen/exaggerate lateral offsets near splits (or draw a zoomed local inset around the player) so the 3 choices are visible.
9. **Route balance**: make the Back Alley genuinely shorter (straighter chord, fewer wiggles) or relabel it; aim for each path to be within ±1 s but with a different risk profile (alley: shorter + bumpy/steps; river: longer + smooth/fast; market: obstacles).
10. Nits: close the gable seam (extend the house box 0.05 m into the prism or drop the prism 0.02 m); add cobble scale variety (smaller cobbles, `roadUV` divisor ~1.2) and a warmer stone tint; reduce daytime fog density on village; check far tree LOD colour.
