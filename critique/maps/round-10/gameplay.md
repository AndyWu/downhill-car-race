## Is the held-wheel correction now the player's own driving?
Mostly yes. The cart stays on the side the player steered to, rides the edge at ~1.1 m with a visible speed cost, and does not cross to the other wall or centre itself. The remaining automatic part is the first ~2-4 s (steer fade) which is acceptable. Fairness: good; it never gives speed beyond the lane's, and a held wheel loses ~25-35 % speed for 6-8 s. Variety between races: real (three races, three different winners/orders: Ace first, human first x2; Vance 2nd/4th/2nd; Ace 2nd/4th/3rd; Maeve 3rd/5th/9th). But the variety is partly accidental pile-ups, not clean skill fights: in 2 of 3 races some AI order inversions exceed what a family would call "fair".
